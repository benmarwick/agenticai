## Proposed text (5 sentences)

The jagged frontier means AI helps with some tasks and harms others, even
when the tasks look equally hard (@DellAcqua2026). In a field experiment with
758 consultants, AI users completed 12.2% more tasks and worked 25.1% faster
with higher quality on tasks inside the frontier (@DellAcqua2026). On a task
outside the frontier, AI users were 19% less likely to produce correct
solutions than consultants without AI (@DellAcqua2026). Benchmark scores can
therefore misstate the value of a system once deployed, because uneven
performance across nearby tasks breaks the link between test scores and field
results (@Gans2026). The practical lesson is to test the agent on each task in
your own workflow, not to trust its average performance.
